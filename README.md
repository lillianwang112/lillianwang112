I'm a sophomore at **Princeton University** studying mathematics, with intended minors in **Neuroscience and OQDS**. My interests are computational neuroscience and optimization, and I aim to pursue a PhD in applied mathematics.

I'm a **research assistant at ZERO Lab**, continuing my summer 2026 internship work on Benders decomposition and Modeling to Generate Alternatives for energy systems optimization.

## Research

### Energy systems optimization · ZERO Lab, Princeton HMEI
**Research intern → research assistant · 2026–present**

Researching Benders decomposition and Modeling to Generate Alternatives in Jesse Jenkins’ group. My summer work included implementing feasibility cuts in MacroEnergy’s Benders workflow, investigating stability and convergence, and adapting MGA from GenX to MacroEnergy to explore distinct decarbonization pathways near the least-cost optimum. I'm continuing this work during the academic year.

### Interpretable deep learning for DNA motif discovery
**Princeton Computer Science & Lewis-Sigler Institute for Integrative Genomics · Dec 2025–Feb 2026**

Adapted L2X (Learning to Explain) to simulated DNA sequences with a fully convolutional selector that preserves base-by-base feature importance. Implemented a custom Gumbel–Softmax sampling layer and a CNN training pipeline, then evaluated motif recovery and whether selected bases reproduce model predictions.

*TensorFlow/Keras · PyTorch · L2X · Model interpretability*

### Smith normal form of matrix powers · PROMYS
**Research Lab · Summer 2025 · Poster presented at JMM 2026**

Studied invariant-factor behavior for specific matrix classes and proved a central conjecture. Presented the work at the 2026 Joint Mathematics Meetings. Attended PROMYS at Boston University in summers 2024 and 2025.

### Triangular Desire in the Age of Algorithmic Companions
**Princeton Research Day 2026 · Nominated presenter**

Applied René Girard’s theory of triangular desire to Reddit discourse on AI companion platforms. Combined seeded LDA, VADER, and emotion and moral lexicons with close reading to identify three discourse clusters in how users narrate relationships with AI. Presented the work at Princeton Research Day.

### Molecular dynamics of amyloid-β
**Course project · Princeton FRS 156 · Spring 2026**

Co-authored a study comparing single-peptide conformations in water and glucose solution using LAMMPS and the CHARMM36m force field. Analyzed radius of gyration and residue contacts across simulation replicates.

## Publications

**Using Machine Learning to Identify Risk Factors for Youth Suicide Attempts**

First author · *Journal of High School Science* · 2024

Compared LightGBM, random forest, and logistic regression on 17,232 CDC survey records, using stratified three-fold cross-validation and feature-importance analysis to investigate factors associated with youth suicide attempts.

**Risk Analysis of the Causes of Food Deserts**

First author · *Actuarial Research Clearing House* · 2024

Combined USDA, Census, and NOAA data with ridge regression, Monte Carlo simulation, and longitudinal analysis to investigate persistent drivers of food-access risk under uncertainty. Our team placed **second nationally in the Modeling the Future Challenge**.
