## About Me

I'm a freshman at **Princeton University** studying **mathematics**, building tools I wish existed and chasing a PhD in applied math. I'm drawn to systems-level problems — energy, computation, policy — and I like writing software that solves real problems for real people.

This summer I'm joining the **[ZERO Lab](https://zero.princeton.edu/)** at Princeton's High Meadows Environmental Institute to build software for energy systems optimization models under Jesse Jenkins.

When I'm not doing math or writing code, I'm probably reading a nice fic or watching Law & Order.

Identified three distinct discourse clusters in how users narrate relationships with AI. Nominated presenter at **Princeton Research Day 2026**.
